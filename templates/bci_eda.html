Interpretation boundary

EDA can reveal where to look, not certify a shift

Visible displacement, wider tails or session-specific channel spread can motivate a formal detector analysis. A descriptive difference is not equivalent to rejecting a statistical null hypothesis, and it does not establish that P(Y|X) stayed unchanged.

Pipeline relationship

Raw trials → FBCSP → PCA → EWMA / Hotelling

This page sits before the detector. It makes the empirical input distributions inspectable before FBCSP and PCA compress them into the monitored representation used by the CSE pipeline.
