Method outline

Three calculations, one change hypothesis.

Follow the same mathematical steps you used in the Heyden derivation.

01 · Reconstruction

Measure the mismatch

Fit PCA on the first concept, reconstruct each observation, and calculate mean squared error (Eq. 1).

02 · Candidate split

Compare two means

For each k, compute the absolute difference between mean losses before and after the split (Eq. 4).

03 · Statistical score

Evaluate the bound

Apply both Bernstein terms (Eq. 5), then select the smallest score across candidates (Eq. 8).
