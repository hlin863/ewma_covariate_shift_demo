Reproduction boundaries and sources

The synthetic generator is adapted conceptually from StreamGenerators.Gaussian. The official experiment configuration is E_synthetic_data.py; the authors' loss visualisation is E_visualize_loss.py.

No automatic data download, streaming retraining, subspace significance test or severity estimate is implemented here. A sample-variance plug-in bound is educational; false-alarm calibration across multiple split positions and monitoring times is not established.
