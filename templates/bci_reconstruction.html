Comparison validity

How to interpret the different methods

The new PCA reconstruction-loss signal evaluates reconstruction mismatch. The previous CSE Table 1 metrics count Stage-I EWMA warnings (CSW) and Stage-II Hotelling confirmations (CSV). Without matched splits, features, PCA settings and ground-truth drift annotations, these are contextual comparisons rather than head-to-head detection-performance claims.
