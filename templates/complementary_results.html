Reading the results

These are Stage I warnings. Without independently annotated shift times, counts on unmodified EEG cannot be called detection rates or false-alarm rates. A warning alone does not establish prediction degradation or a need to retrain.

A residual-only warning needs a Stage II representation that retains the changed information; validating only PC1 could miss the same change. Compare this with controlled shifts and classifier outcomes in later experiments.
