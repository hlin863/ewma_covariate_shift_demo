Research problem

EEG distributions change while the intended task relationship can remain stable.

Ptrain(X)≠Ptest(X)
Ptrain(Y|X)=Ptest(Y|X)

The detector asks when the data have shifted; the longer-term research asks when that evidence justifies adaptation.

Current boundaryCSE warning and validation are implemented and testable.

The full CSE-UAEL loop—pseudo-labelling, dynamic ensembles and adaptation after confirmation—is future work, not a completed claim.
