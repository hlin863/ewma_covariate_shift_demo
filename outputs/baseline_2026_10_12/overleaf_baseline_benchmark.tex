\subsection{Preserved EWMA Baseline: Evidence and Reproducibility}
\label{sec:ewma_baseline}
\paragraph{Source revision.} This archival baseline indexes two distinct cases at GitHub commit \texttt{53bf9fa53a22da2fafbe881d888537b7e2ed426b}: synthetic D2 SD-EWMA with two-window K--S validation and BCI IV 2B participant B01 PC1 EWMA with intended Hotelling $T^2$ validation. They are not one jointly rerun experiment. See \url{https://github.com/hlin863/ewma_covariate_shift_demo/blob/53bf9fa/outputs/baseline_2026_10_12/README.md}.
\paragraph{Effective parameters.} For D2, the saved summary identifies $\lambda=0.4$, a final 1000-point Table III window with reference changes at 4101--4901 in steps of 100, and Stage-II windows of 10 before and 10 after. The exact seed, run command and all parameters are not archived. For B01 (04E/05E), saved event rows establish $\lambda=0.28$, $L=1.96$, variance update mode \texttt{always} and variance smoothing 0.05; historical Stage-II effective settings are not established. Current runner defaults must not be substituted for historical parameters.

\paragraph{D2 evidence.} The event file \url{https://github.com/hlin863/ewma_covariate_shift_demo/blob/53bf9fa/outputs/metrics/paper2015/d2/stage2_validations.csv} distinguishes evaluated, skipped and ineligible warnings. In the saved \url{https://github.com/hlin863/ewma_covariate_shift_demo/blob/53bf9fa/outputs/metrics/paper2015/d2/summary.json}, computed TSSD-EWMA FP/FN/RCI/CT are 0.2018\%, 55.5556\%, 19.75 and 0.1460 s, compared with saved published references of 0\%, 11.1\%, 10 and 0.224 s. This is not a numerical reproduction.
\paragraph{B01 evidence.} The 17 Stage-I warning indices are $37,48,51,66,76,85,94,116,125,139,146,159,197,219,239,276,300$ (12 in 04E and 5 in 05E). The historical B01-only aggregate records 280 training and 320 testing trials and 14 published versus 17 computed warnings. Source: \url{https://github.com/hlin863/ewma_covariate_shift_demo/blob/53bf9fa/outputs/metrics/bci_2b_stage1_warnings.csv}.
\paragraph{Unresolved Hotelling outcome.} The \url{https://github.com/hlin863/ewma_covariate_shift_demo/blob/53bf9fa/outputs/metrics/bci_table1_comparison.csv} aggregate gives \texttt{computed\_csv=0}, whereas \url{https://github.com/hlin863/ewma_covariate_shift_demo/blob/53bf9fa/outputs/metrics/bci_2b_table1_results.csv} gives \texttt{computed\_csv=5}. Neither is supported by archived timestamped Hotelling event rows. Do not label 17 warnings as rejected or confirmed. The full frozen experiment still requires checksums, dependency versions, exact command, effective feature dimension, validation windows and individual Stage-II outcomes. New runs should be archived separately without overwriting the original results.
